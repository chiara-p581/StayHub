Frequently Asked Questions

For most flexible rates, you can cancel your reservation free of charge up to 48 hours before your scheduled check-in time. Cancellations made within 48 hours will incur a fee equal to the first night's room rate. Non-refundable rates cannot be cancelled or modified.

Standard check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. We are happy to accommodate early check-in or late check-out requests based on availability. Please contact the front desk on the day of your arrival or departure to check status. Additional fees may apply.

Yes, we are a pet-friendly boutique hotel! We welcome well-behaved dogs up to 40 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $50 per stay applies. Please let us know in advance if you plan to bring your furry friend so we can prepare some welcome treats.
