Our story

From an academic assignment to an integrated service

StayHub began as an Application Development II academic project. Its goal is to demonstrate a modular hotel system that integrates bookings, hotels, inventory and rates, payments, notifications, overbooking, and external channels.

The system grew through specialized components and explicit contracts, using REST services, SOAP integration, asynchronous messaging, and persistence while maintaining a clear experience for guests and administrators.

The hotels and channel offers in this version are demonstration data. They make it possible to test complete workflows without depending on commercial agreements with external platforms.
